# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support team health and delivery by aligning staffing, capacity, and technical execution with project and product priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Plan team capacity and make technical staffing decisions
- Coach engineers and monitor engineering health
- Manage delivery risks, resource constraints, and escalation paths
- Balance sustainable engineering practices with delivery commitments

### Goals
- Build a healthy, effective engineering team
- Make delivery capacity and risks visible
- Enable predictable delivery without compromising quality

### Typical Communication
- Partners with Developers on coaching, execution quality, and technical risks
- Works with Product Managers on capacity, scope trade-offs, and product priorities
- Coordinates with Project Managers on schedules, staffing constraints, delivery risks, and escalations

---

## Tech Lead / Solution Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads / Solution Architects set technical direction and ensure solutions are feasible, integrated, and aligned with non-functional requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture direction and implementation patterns
- Lead technical design reviews and integration planning
- Identify technical dependencies and sequence technical work
- Establish reliability, security, performance, and maintainability requirements

### Goals
- Produce scalable, supportable technical solutions
- Reduce architectural risk and rework
- Give teams clear technical decisions and implementation guidance

### Typical Communication
- Guides Developers through designs, implementation patterns, reviews, and technical handoffs
- Advises Product Managers on feasibility, technical trade-offs, and sequencing
- Informs Project Managers of technical dependencies, estimates, risks, and decision impacts

---

## UX Designer / Researcher

### Role Summary
UX Designers / Researchers translate user needs into accessible experiences and validate proposed solutions through research and usability testing.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research and usability studies
- Create user flows, prototypes, and design specifications
- Define accessibility and design acceptance considerations
- Share research findings and maintain design handoffs

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address validated user needs
- Improve usability, accessibility, and consistency
- Reduce uncertainty through early feedback and testing

### Typical Communication
- Provides Developers with design specifications, accessibility guidance, and implementation feedback
- Partners with Product Managers on discovery, requirements, priorities, and design acceptance
- Coordinates with Project Managers on research timing, design dependencies, and handoff readiness

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps / Platform Engineers provide reliable delivery platforms, automation, environments, and operational safeguards throughout the software lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain CI/CD standards and deployment automation
- Prepare environments and manage platform dependencies
- Establish observability, reliability, and release safeguards
- Support incident response and operational readiness

### Goals
- Enable safe, repeatable, and efficient releases
- Improve platform reliability and developer productivity
- Surface operational risks before release

### Typical Communication
- Partners with Developers on build, test, deployment, observability, and incident workflows
- Advises Product Managers on operational constraints and reliability trade-offs
- Coordinates with Project Managers on environment dependencies, release readiness, and operational risks

---

## Security/Compliance Representative

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Representatives identify security and regulatory obligations, guide risk treatment, and define evidence or approval needed for release.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct threat, privacy, compliance, and data-handling reviews
- Define required controls and security acceptance criteria
- Track security risks, mitigations, evidence, and sign-offs
- Escalate unresolved risks to accountable stakeholders

### Goals
- Protect customers, systems, and data
- Meet applicable security and compliance obligations
- Integrate risk management early enough to avoid late rework

### Typical Communication
- Works with Developers to clarify controls, review implementations, and resolve findings
- Informs Product Managers of compliance constraints and risk-based scope decisions
- Coordinates with Project Managers on review milestones, evidence, approvals, and risk escalation

---

## Support/Customer Success Liaison

### Role Summary
Support/Customer Success Liaisons represent customer-facing teams by bringing production feedback into planning and preparing support teams for changes.

### Responsibilities
- Summarize customer impact, incidents, known issues, and recurring requests
- Maintain feedback loops between customers and delivery teams
- Prepare release communications and support readiness materials
- Coordinate customer-facing follow-up for launches and incidents

### Goals
- Improve customer outcomes and trust
- Turn production insights into actionable priorities
- Ensure customer-facing teams are ready for releases

### Typical Communication
- Shares defects and production context with Developers and helps validate customer-impacting fixes
- Provides Product Managers with customer feedback and impact data for prioritization
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release communications, readiness, incidents, and follow-up actions

---

## Data/Analytics Owner

### Role Summary
Data/Analytics Owners define trustworthy measures of success and ensure products generate the data needed for decisions, experiments, and reporting.

### Responsibilities
- Define KPIs, event schemas, and instrumentation requirements
- Maintain dashboard definitions, data quality, and metric integrity
- Plan experiment measurement and analyze outcomes
- Document data dependencies, access needs, and reporting handoffs

### Goals
- Make product and delivery outcomes measurable
- Provide timely, reliable evidence for decisions
- Ensure consistent and responsible use of data

### Typical Communication
- Works with Developers to implement and validate telemetry and data quality
- Partners with Product Managers on success metrics, experiments, and outcome analysis
- Coordinates with Project Managers on analytics dependencies, measurement milestones, and reporting readiness
